The touring artist profile is moving in the right direction visually, but it still feels slightly too much like a premium dashboard instead of a cinematic editorial experience

I want the touring profile pages to feel:

- immersive
- emotional
- cinematic
- luxurious
- atmospheric
- storytelling-driven

NOT:
- admin panel
- sports stats site
- finance dashboard
- SaaS analytics page

IMPORTANT REFINEMENTS:

1. LESS “BOXED UI”
Right now too many sections feel trapped inside rectangles/cards

Reduce:
- harsh borders
- repetitive containers
- visible grid feeling

Instead:
- let sections breathe
- allow imagery to bleed into layouts
- overlap layers naturally
- create asymmetrical compositions
- float typography over photography

2. MORE FULLSCREEN CINEMATIC MOMENTS
Add sections where:
- giant concert photography dominates the screen
- artist silhouettes blend into darkness
- crowd lights/glow fill the background
- typography overlays the image dramatically

I want moments that feel like:
- a Netflix concert documentary
- Apple keynote transitions
- luxury music editorial design

3. BIGGER VISUAL STORYTELLING
Not every section needs stats

Some sections should simply create emotion and atmosphere

Example:
fullscreen crowd image with giant typography:
“MÉXICO EN LOS ESCENARIOS DEL MUNDO”

Minimal UI in those moments

4. DATA SHOULD FEEL CURATED
Do NOT make the page feel like a complete database dump

Instead:
- reveal stats gradually
- prioritize hero numbers
- use oversized typography for key milestones
- create “single-stat moments”

Example:
one fullscreen section dedicated only to:
“$90M EN TAQUILLA”

with cinematic lighting and subtle supporting text

5. TOUR MAP SHOULD FEEL EMOTIONAL
Not like Google Maps

The map should feel:
- atmospheric
- glowing
- artistic
- documentary-like

Think:
glowing routing lines
dark environments
city lights
movement between Mexico and the United States

6. MORE PHOTOGRAPHY THROUGHOUT THE PAGE
Use:
- large concert imagery
- stage lighting
- backstage moments
- silhouettes
- crowd shots
- texture overlays
- smoke/fog/grain

The page currently needs more atmosphere and movement

7. REDUCE “DASHBOARD ENERGY”
Current structure is strong, but visually it still leans too much toward:
“premium analytics site”

I want:
“cinematic music culture platform”

The emotional experience should dominate first
The analytics should support it second

Ideal balance:
70% cinematic editorial
30% data UI
