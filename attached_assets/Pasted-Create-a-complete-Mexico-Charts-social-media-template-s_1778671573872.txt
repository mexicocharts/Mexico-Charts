Create a complete Mexico Charts social media template system for Instagram/Facebook posts.

Goal: make reusable premium templates that match the Mexico Charts brand: cinematic, luxury, editorial, black background, neon emerald green accents, soft white/gray text, dramatic lighting, subtle grain/glow, modern music-platform energy. Think Spotify Wrapped + Apple keynote + Billboard chart authority, not esports, not generic analytics dashboard, not colorful clutter.

Use the existing Mexico Charts logo/branding. Do not redesign the logo.

Create templates in Instagram 4:5 vertical format first, with versions that can also adapt to square if needed.

Templates needed:

1. Daily Top Songs
- Header: “TOP CANCIONES DIARIAS”
- Platform/source label area: Spotify / Apple Music / YouTube / Deezer
- Date area
- Ranking list for Top 10
- Keep artist photos optional/off by default so the account does not look like a fan page for the same #1 artist every day
- Make the #1 feel premium but not oversized in a cheesy way

2. Daily Top Artists
- Header: “TOP ARTISTAS DIARIOS”
- Rank, artist, movement, streams/listeners if available
- Clean list format
- Optional subtle artist silhouette/photo area, not required

3. Weekly Top Songs
- Header: “TOP CANCIONES SEMANALES”
- Bigger editorial layout than daily posts
- Include rank movement, peak, weeks if available
- More premium and “official chart” looking

4. Weekly Top Albums
- Header: “TOP ÁLBUMES SEMANALES”
- Include album cover area
- Top 5 or Top 10 format
- Make covers look cinematic with shadows/glow, not plain boxes

5. Viral Songs
- Header: “VIRAL MÉXICO”
- More energetic design, but still Mexico Charts premium
- Use green glow, motion lines, blurred background textures
- Avoid neon rainbow colors

6. Artist Milestone Post
- Example headline: “[ARTISTA] supera [NÚMERO] streams”
- Large dramatic headline
- Artist image area
- Supporting stat line
- Small source/date area
- Good for achievements, records, and milestones

7. Industry/Market Insight
- Example headline: “México entra al Top 10 global”
- Editorial/report style
- Big stat number
- Short context paragraph
- Source area
- Should feel like a premium music-business report, not a chart card

8. Touring Data Post
- Headline: “[TOUR] recauda [GROSS]”
- Include gross, tickets, shows, average gross if available
- Use concert/stage-inspired background
- Source/date area
- Make it feel powerful and cinematic

9. Quote/Headline Post
- Big oversized typography
- Minimal design
- For bold statements, announcements, and captions
- No unnecessary boxes

10. Carousel Cover Template
- Strong cover slide for Instagram carousel
- Big cinematic title
- Mexico Charts logo
- Date/source
- Designed to make people swipe

11. Carousel Data Slide Template
- For slides 2–8
- Reusable layout for chart facts, bullet insights, small rankings, artist comparisons, or source notes
- Consistent with cover design but slightly cleaner

12. Final CTA Slide
- Text examples:
  - “Sigue a Mexico Charts para más datos de la música mexicana”
  - “Datos. Cultura. Impacto.”
  - “México en los charts globales”

Design rules:
- Black/dark background
- Emerald green as the only strong accent color
- White and gray text only
- No gold
- No random colors
- No esports style
- No generic dashboard cards
- No overuse of boxes
- Use big typography, cinematic spacing, subtle depth, glows, shadows, gradients, atmospheric grain
- Avoid clutter
- Avoid periods at the end of short stylistic headlines
- Spanish text only on the templates
- Keep source/date areas clean and small
- Every template must feel like part of the same Mexico Charts system
