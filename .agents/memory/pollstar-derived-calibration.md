---
name: Pollstar derived calibration policy
description: Privacy and licensing boundary for purchased Pollstar-informed touring estimates.
---

Use purchased Pollstar material only as private derived aggregates or aggregate calibration profiles. Never retain or expose report text, source rows, show dates, paid-ticket counts, gross, sell-through, or sold-out fields from an individual Pollstar report.

**Why:** The purchased source is licensed reference material, not application data. The Touring Estimation Lab may use its derived signals while preserving a strict no-raw-storage boundary.

**How to apply:** Keep provenance private and explicitly mark raw-row storage as false. Exact-venue calibration may retain only the minimum derived parameters required by the model (for example, a capacity anchor, historical price signal, and sample size), with one deterministic profile per artist and normalized venue.
